Why Riverton still needs its Thursday market

Mark Ellison argues that a market is more than a place to buy cabbages.

By Mark Ellison · Riverton · 2026-11-13

Every Thursday for nearly eight hundred years, farmers and makers have set up on Market Square, and every few years someone suggests we do without it. This year the suggestion is that the square would earn more as a car park.

The market makes little profit for the council, that much is true. But it is where an elderly man who lives alone speaks to someone before lunch. It is where a teenager sells her first handmade candles. It is where the butcher knows which of his customers prefers the thinner slice.

Economists talk about footfall and spend. Those matter. They are not why people come. They come because a market is the one place in town where being there is the whole point. Keep it.
