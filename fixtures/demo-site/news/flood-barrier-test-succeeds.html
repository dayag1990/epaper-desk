Flood barrier passes first full test on the Wren

The new demountable wall held back a simulated surge for six hours.

By Tom Ashworth · Riverton, 5 Nov · 2026-11-05

Riverton's new demountable flood barrier held back a simulated surge for six hours on Wednesday, passing the first full-scale test since it was installed along Quay Road this summer.

Environment Agency staff and volunteers bolted 140 aluminium panels into place in under ninety minutes. Pumps then raised the water behind the wall to 1.8 metres, a level last seen in the floods of 2019.

Project manager Priya Nair said no leaks were detected at the joints, and the seepage rate measured at the base was well below the design limit. The barrier will be tested again in January with the river running at its normal winter flow.

Residents on Quay Road, who were evacuated twice in the last decade, watched from the pavement. Several said the quiet was the most striking thing: no sandbags, no lorries, just the sound of pumps.
