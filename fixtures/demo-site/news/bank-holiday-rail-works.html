Rail firm confirms three weekends of line closures for signal upgrade

Replacement buses will run between Riverton and Marlow while engineers work.

By Olivia Marsh · Riverton, 19 Nov · 2026-11-19

Trains between Riverton and Marlow will not run on three weekends in January and February while engineers replace ageing signalling equipment, the rail operator has confirmed.

The closures will take place on 17-18 January, 31 January-1 February and 14-15 February. Replacement buses will run every thirty minutes, adding about twenty-five minutes to the journey. The operator said it chose winter weekends to limit the impact on commuters and the Saturday market.

The £14 million upgrade will replace signal boxes dating from the 1960s with a modern system that can be run from a regional control centre. The operator says it should reduce delays caused by equipment failure, which accounted for almost a third of late trains on the line last year.

Passenger groups have welcomed the investment but asked for clearer communication. Details of replacement services will appear on station boards and the operator's website from next week.
