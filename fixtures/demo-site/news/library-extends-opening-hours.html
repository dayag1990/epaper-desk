Central Library to open on Sundays from December

Volunteers and a new grant make seven-day service possible for the first time in a decade.

By Grace Linton · Riverton, 6 Nov · 2026-11-06

Riverton Central Library will open on Sundays from the first weekend of December, ending a decade in which the building was closed for one day in every seven.

The extra hours, from 11am to 4pm, are funded by a £48,000 community grant and staffed by a rota of 60 trained volunteers working alongside two paid librarians. Library manager Joseph Adeyemi said the first Sundays would focus on the children's floor and the computer suite, which are the most requested services.

The library recorded 212,000 visits last year, up 9 per cent. Adeyemi said surveys of users showed that people who work weekdays were the most frustrated by the old timetable.

Half the people we asked said they simply could not come on a weekday. Sunday gives them a way in.

The trial will run until June. If visits meet the target of 150 a day, the council has indicated it will look for money to make the arrangement permanent.
