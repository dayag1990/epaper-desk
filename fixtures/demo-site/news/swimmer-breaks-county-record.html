Teenager from Riverton breaks county 200m record

Aisha Rahman, 16, shaves nearly two seconds off the mark set in 2012.

By Sam Pritchard · Riverton, 9 Nov · 2026-11-09

Sixteen-year-old Aisha Rahman broke the county 200 metres freestyle record at the Riverton Aquatic Centre on Sunday, finishing in 2:03.14 and taking almost two seconds off a mark that had stood since 2012.

Rahman, who trains with Wren Valley Swimming Club six mornings a week before school, said she did not look at the clock until she touched the wall. Her coach, Neil Fairbrother, said the result showed what disciplined work could do.

She now has a qualifying time for the regional championships in February.

Fairbrother said the club would use the winter to work on her turns, which he believes are worth another half second, and that she will swim the 400 metres at the next county gala. "She has not yet reached her ceiling," he said.
