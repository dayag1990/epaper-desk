Wren Valley Brewery to double output with new site

Forty jobs promised at a converted grain store beside the railway.

By Olivia Marsh · Riverton, 11 Nov · 2026-11-11

Wren Valley Brewery has agreed to lease a disused grain store beside the railway line and plans to double its output by the end of next year, creating around forty jobs.

Founded in a farm shed in 2014, the company now employs 31 people and supplies pubs across three counties. Managing director Fiona Calder said demand for its pale ale and a new low-alcohol lager had outgrown the current site.

The grain store will house new tanks, a canning line and a taproom open to the public. Planning permission was granted in October with conditions on delivery times, designed to protect nearby homes from early-morning noise.

Calder said recruitment would start in spring and that she expected most roles to be filled locally, including trainee brewers and packaging operatives.
