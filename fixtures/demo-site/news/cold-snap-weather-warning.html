Frost warning issued as cold snap settles over the valley

Temperatures could fall to minus six overnight on Thursday and Friday.

By Tom Ashworth · Riverton, 18 Nov · 2026-11-18

A yellow weather warning for ice has been issued for the Wren Valley from Thursday evening, with temperatures forecast to fall as low as minus six on higher ground around the town.

The council said gritters would treat main roads from 6pm and that salt bins in residential streets had been refilled this week. Drivers are advised to allow extra time and to check on elderly neighbours.

The cold snap is expected to ease on Sunday as milder air arrives from the west.

Farmers have been asked to bring livestock closer to shelter, and the Riverton Food Bank has appealed for donations of warm clothing and blankets after a rise in requests during the first frosts. Anyone who needs shelter can call the council helpline.
