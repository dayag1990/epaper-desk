Friday night market returns to Tanners Quay

Sixty stalls, live music and a hot-chocolate trail promised for the winter season.

By Grace Linton · Riverton, 7 Nov · 2026-11-07

The Friday night market will return to Tanners Quay this month with sixty stalls, a covered food court and a programme of live music that organisers say is the biggest in its eight-year history.

Stallholders include returning favourites such as the Wren Valley cheese van and a bakery that sells out of cardamom buns within the hour, alongside twelve first-time traders from the Riverton Makers Hub.

A hot-chocolate trail will link five cafés along the quay. Visitors collect a stamp at each and can win a hamper at the final stop. The market opens at 5pm on 14 November and runs every Friday until 19 December.

Parking will be limited, and organisers are encouraging visitors to use the free shuttle from the railway station car park.
